iphone-duo-transition

Three.js reconstruction of the iPhone Duo fold transition. Each display is a stencil over a stationary image plane registered to it; a tracing-paper scattering model blurs and darkens what it reveals. Detail energy and optical flow are measured in screen space from 0° to 180°.
